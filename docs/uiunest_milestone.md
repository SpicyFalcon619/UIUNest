## 🏗️ What We Built
We didn't just build a simple website; we engineered a **comprehensive ecosystem** for university students and landlords:
1. **Housing Discovery Engine:** Leaflet maps, zone filters, and detailed property views.
2. **Flatmate Compatibility Matrix:** A complex matching algorithm scoring users based on sleep, diet, noise, cleanliness, and guest policies.
3. **Peer-to-Peer Marketplace:** A full exchange system for students to buy, sell, and negotiate offers with real-time UI states.
4. **Mess Bill Manager:** A complete financial splitting tool for roommates to track rent and utility bills equitably.
5. **Master Admin Dashboard:** A God-mode control center for approving verifications, handling user complaints, and monitoring system-wide analytics.
6. **Authentication & Roles:** Dedicated experiences and permissions for Students, Landlords, and Admins.

## ✨ The Polish (The 99% to 100% phase)
Getting a functional app is one thing, but making it feel **premium** was our recent focus:
- **Flawless Mobile Responsiveness:** Fixed horizontal scrolling bugs, protected the bottom-nav floating pill, and ensured custom dropdowns flex perfectly on tiny phone screens.
- **Dynamic Navbar Behaviors:** Engineered a sleek auto-hide transition on sub-pages to maximize screen real estate, while keeping it permanently anchored on the homepage.
- **Copywriting:** Replaced robotic AI phrasing with warm, conversational, human-centric marketing copy.
- **Micro-Aesthetics:** Dialed in exact `border-radius` variables (hello, 12px squircles!), balanced whitespace, and unified typography across the entire platform.

## 🎯 Fun Facts
- **Most Complex System:** The Flatmate Matching Engine. We had to dig deep to fix caching issues so that when a user updated their sleep schedule, the compatibility scores across the entire platform recalculated instantly.
- **The "Squircle" Rule:** We specifically avoided perfect `50px` pill shapes for selected navbar items in favor of a `12px` radius to perfectly match the design language of our cards and modals.
- **Commit 195:** The final commit that brought us to v1.2.0 was stripping out emojis from the documentation to ensure a highly professional open-source presentation!
